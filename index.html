What we stand for.

Principles / 04
001

Provenance over hype.

Every certified product can trace its intelligence to a source. Models, data, decisions — accountable by design, not by accident.

002

Trust is infrastructure.

The mark is the rail. Companies invest in the badge because consumers reward it. A flywheel built on substance, not signal.

003

Independence, always.

We are neither a model lab nor a platform. We answer to the registry and the public — the only way certification holds value.

004

Clarity is kindness.

Plain-language disclosure. Visible badges. Public audit trails. The opposite of the black box — by deliberate, ongoing choice.
